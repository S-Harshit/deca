# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (version 2.1, CC BY 4.0), summarised here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent, and give and take feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Keep discussion on topic, and keep other people's private information private.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through GitHub (a private message, or a report using GitHub's own tools). Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
